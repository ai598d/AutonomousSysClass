DESCRIPTION:
This course provides an introduction to Industrial Automation using Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC). Participants will gain an understanding of system modeling and simulation, automation processes, PLC system architecture, ladder logic programming, data acquisition and system design, safety & reliability. Miscellaneous topics will be covered if time permits!

During class lectures, students will learn fundamentals of system automation. Lectures will be focused towards developing engineering judgment through conceptual understanding and simulation. There will be a homework after each lecture to solidify the important topics covered during the lecture. In the lab portion, students are required to independently complete foundational lab exercises to develop hands-on skills. Subsequently, participants will form teams to undertake a collaborative final project and presentation.

COURSE LEARNING OUTCOMES:
Upon completion of this course, students should be able to:
1. Apply fundamental ideas of system modeling and simulation

2. Explain the operation of motor and basic electrical components
3. Apply fundamental concepts of digital logic.
4. Understand programmable logic controller (PLC) architecture and manufacturer datasheet.

5. Understand ladder logic.
6. Implementing control schemes with hardware, especially PLCs
7. Apply fundamental concepts of automation system design, safety and reliability.
